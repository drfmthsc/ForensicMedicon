Abstract Submission

Free papers and e-posters for FORENSIC MEDICON 2027 — the 48th Annual National Conference of the Indian Academy of Forensic Medicine, 2–6 February 2027.

Register first — the link comes by email

Abstracts are open. Submission is through a Google Form, and the link is sent to you by email once your registration is complete.

The email carries your registration ID, which is a required field on the submission form. Only registered delegates may submit.

Eligibility

  1. You must be a registered delegate before you submit. Registration is not accepted after submission — the registration ID is a required field on the form.
  2. One presentation per presenting author across the whole conference. You may submit one abstract only, whether oral or poster. There is no limit on co-authorship.
  3. Maximum five authors, including the presenting author. The presenting author is underlined.
  4. A valid IAFM membership number is required for competitive entry in the Postgraduate and Faculty streams. Entries without one are moved to the non-competitive track.
  5. Work must be original and must not have been presented at a previous national conference.
  6. An Institutional Ethics Committee approval number must be stated where human subjects, records or identifiable material are involved.

Abstract format

Length & structure

  • 250 words, excluding title, authors and affiliations
  • Structured — Background · Methods · Results · Conclusion
  • Case reports — Introduction · Case · Discussion · Conclusion

Title & authors

  • Title 25 words maximum, no abbreviations
  • Maximum five authors; presenting author underlined
  • Institution and city for each author

Other requirements

  • Three keywords
  • English
  • Times New Roman 12 pt, single spaced
  • Entered directly into the submission form

Canvas

Build one page at 92.7 × 52.1 cm (36.5 × 20.5 in) — a 16:9 rectangle, so it fills the screen with no letterboxing. The templates below are already set to this size; if you build your own, set Design → Slide Size → Custom to these dimensions and export as PDF.

Minimum type sizes

Measured on the 92.7 cm canvas. They look large because the canvas is large — when you zoom to a third of the width, body text reads at about 35 pt on screen, comfortably legible from the back of a hall.

What fits

At 32 pt across three columns this canvas holds roughly 650 words plus two figures or tables — more than a printed A0 poster carries. Write for the space rather than pasting in the full paper.

  • Three columns, reading left to right, each a self-contained section
  • Group content so that one zoom shows one complete section — this is what makes the poster presentable
  • Light background, dark text — the halls are lit for presentation
  • Figures at 150 dpi or better at final size, so they hold up when magnified
  • Single page, under 20 MB, fonts embedded — PowerPoint does this automatically on File → Save as → PDF

Naming your file

Name the file with your registration ID and nothing else — lowercase, no spaces, no surname, no version number.

fm27-pg-042.pdf

The submission form shows you the exact filename to use, generated from your registration ID. Copy it rather than typing it.

This matters on the day. Files named by registration ID sort into running order by themselves, so the desk in your hall opens the next file down the list instead of hunting through two hundred documents called final poster. The same rule applies to oral presentation slides.

Presenting with zoom

Rehearse the zoom before you travel. You will have four minutes and the venue mouse. Open the PDF in full screen, practise moving between sections, and know your zoom level before you stand up. A presenter who is still hunting for the Results panel at ninety seconds has lost the room — and slide quality carries fifteen marks.

  • Open the PDF in Adobe Acrobat Reader and press Ctrl + L for full screen
  • Zoom with Ctrl + and Ctrl −; move with the scroll wheel or by dragging
  • Start on the full poster so the audience sees the whole structure, then zoom section by section
  • Return to the full view for your conclusion

Selection & judging

Abstracts are scored blind by the Scientific Committee. Nothing is rejected outright — an entry not shortlisted for the competitive track is automatically offered a place in the non-competitive track.

  • Three judges per panel; the median of the three scores is taken.
  • A judge does not assess an entry from their own institution.
  • Tiebreak — the higher Scientific Content sub-score.
  • Score sheets are retained by the Scientific Committee.

Results of the competitive tracks are announced at valediction. The awards conferred by the Indian Academy of Forensic Medicine at its annual conference are separate, and are administered by the Academy.

Before you upload

Run through this once. Most problems on the day come from one of these six.

  • One page, 92.7 × 52.1 cm, exported as PDF — not PowerPoint
  • Body text at 32 pt or larger. Open the PDF, zoom to a third of the width, and read it from across the room
  • Figures sharp when magnified. Zoom in on every image — if it blurs, replace it with a higher-resolution version
  • Named by registration ID, lowercase, nothing else
  • Under 20 MB, fonts embedded, no password or security applied to the PDF
  • Rehearsed — full screen, moving section to section, inside four minutes
